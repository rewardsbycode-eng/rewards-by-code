   base in sitemap.xml to match. All in-site links are relative and
   need no change.

   source post's "$50 back when you spend $50" promotion says "Ends
   March 31st" — long past. The page carries Rakuten's standing facts
   and his referral link, and says promotions change. Alexander: if a
   current Rakuten sign-up bonus exists, add its amount and terms.

   grounds only the setup (MSI machine, three-monitor desk, Chromebook
   writing desk, phone). No product names or Amazon links were
   invented. Alexander fills the list with the exact products and his
   amzn.to links, or deletes the section.

   home for every post; flagged here for Alexander to move if he

     work" subsection (they are earning apps with his codes).
   - Acorns → Apps That Pay (an app with a $5 referral bonus).
   - Crypto.com sign-up post → Crypto Systems (a crypto exchange
     bonus, alongside the Coinbase system).

     referral disclosed on the spot; the Money Guides intro states
     plainly that the first four guides carry no affiliate links and
     these two do.
