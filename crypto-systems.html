Deep dive: the DCA bot system

My Sovereign DCA guide is the technical blueprint: a Dollar Cost Averaging system across the same three layers — exchange (Coinbase) → decision (strategy) → execution (3Commas) — with the focus on API latency, risk logic, and execution precision.

  • Exchange layer: I use Coinbase Advanced for its REST/WebSocket infrastructure. Standard interfaces suffer slippage and rate-limiting; Advanced reduces fees by up to 50% and keeps orders executing during volatility spikes.
  • The #1 cause of bot failure is HTTP 429: Too Many Requests — Coinbase limits REST calls to roughly 10 per second. The fix is exponential backoff: retry logic that waits longer after every failure, which preserves your IP reputation.
  • Execution engine: 3Commas handles the DCA math — safety orders and volume scales — 24/7, without emotional interference.
  • Judge a bot by drawdown, not ROI. A bot with 50% ROI but 40% max drawdown is structurally fragile; a 15% ROI bot with 5% max drawdown is sustainable. My extension: add a volatility filter — pause safety orders if the 14-day ATR exceeds 10% of the asset price. (Background reading: the 3Commas DCA backtesting guide.)
